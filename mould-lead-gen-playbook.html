1. The Strategy in One Page

The big idea: Mould is the perfect lead magnet because the urgency is real — it harms health, it spreads fast, and for landlords it's now a legal duty. You lead with a free survey (low commitment = more leads), then convert the survey into a fixed-price job.

The offerWhy it works
Free, no-obligation mould & damp survey + fixed-price quotePeople won't book "removal" from an ad, but they'll book a free check. The survey is where you build trust and close.

6. The Lead Form (keep it short)

Ask only what you need to call them back and price the job. Fewer questions = more leads.

  1. Name
  2. Phone number
  3. Postcode / area
  4. Are you a homeowner or landlord?
  5. Where's the mould? (bathroom / bedroom / wall / ceiling / multiple rooms)
  6. How long has it been there? (optional — signals urgency)
  7. Best time to call you

8. Turning Leads Into Jobs (this is where money is made)

The golden rule: call within the hour. Lead value drops fast. A 5-minute callback beats a competitor who calls tomorrow.
  • Call first, text as backup: "Hi [name], it's [you] from Lyon Renovations about your free mould survey — when suits for me to pop round?"
  • Book the survey on that call. Give a date/time then and there.
  • At the survey: show the cause, explain the fix, hand over a clear fixed-price quote. Offer to start within X days.
  • Follow up any "thinking about it" leads after 2 days and again after a week.

10. Video Ad Idea (full concept + script)

Title: "It's Not Just a Stain"  |  Length: 20–30 seconds  |  Where: Facebook/Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
Style: shot on a phone, real and raw beats polished. Vertical (9:16).

The hook (first 3 seconds are everything)

Open on an extreme close-up of black mould creeping up a corner, slow zoom. Bold on-screen text: "You think it's just a stain…"

Shot-by-shot script

TimeWhat we seeVoiceover / on-screen text
0–3sClose-up of black mould in a corner, slow zoom inTEXT: "You think it's just a stain..."
3–7sQuick cuts: mould behind a bed, around a window, on a ceilingVO: "But mould spreads — and the spores get into the air your family breathes."
7–12sYou (or your team) arriving, clean van, doing a survey with a damp meterVO: "We find the real cause, not just the patch."
12–18sSatisfying before → after: mouldy wall transforms to clean, fresh wallVO: "Treated properly, so it doesn't come back."
18–25sYou smiling to camera, logo, phone numberVO: "Book your free survey this week." TEXT: Free Survey · Fixed Price · [Phone]

Why this works

  • Pattern interrupt: the gross close-up + "just a stain" stops the scroll.
  • Problem → agitate → solve: classic structure in under 30 seconds.
  • The before/after is the most shareable, trust-building moment — lean into it.
  • Real you on camera = local trust, which big firms can't fake.
Make 3 versions of the hook and run them against each other. Same video, different first 3 seconds: (1) "You think it's just a stain..."   (2) "This costs UK families thousands every year..."   (3) "Landlords — this could get you fined." Keep the winner.
