# Google Business Profile Setup — Lyon Renovations

**Goal of this document:** set up the Google Business Profile (GBP) as a bulletproof, conversion-optimised base that generates the maximum number of **calls and enquiries**. This is the internal foundation. External work (review campaigns, weekly posts, per-town landing pages, cold outreach) comes *after* this is 100% complete.

This brief is self-contained — everything needed to execute is here. Copy text exactly where provided.

## Business context

- **Business:** Lyon Renovations (Lyon Construction Ltd)
- **Base:** Ely, Cambridgeshire, UK — service-area business (we travel to the client)
- **What we do:** complete property renovations & refurbishments — residential + commercial

## The marketing logic (why this gets enquiries)

Three levers decide whether the profile ranks AND converts:

1. **Get found** — primary category + services + service areas (relevance to the search)
2. **Build trust at a glance** — photos, reviews, "free quotes", complete profile (the decision happens in ~5 seconds)
3. **Make contact frictionless** — call button, messaging, quote link all live

Every task below serves one of these. The five highest-impact items for enquiries are flagged 🎯.
