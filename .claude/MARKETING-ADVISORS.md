# Your 7 Marketing Advisors

This project includes **7 specialist marketing advisors**, each an expert in one area.
They're powered by the [marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)
collection (44 skills) by Corey Haines, copied into `.claude/skills/`.

## How to use them

Just ask Claude in plain language — it will route you to the right advisor automatically.
You can also call one by name, e.g. *"Ask the **copy** advisor to write me a homepage headline"*.

| Advisor | Talk to it about | Covers |
|---------|------------------|--------|
| **strategy** | Where to start, the big picture, a full plan | marketing-plan, marketing-ideas, marketing-psychology, customer-research, product-marketing |
| **seo-content** | Getting found on Google & AI search | seo-audit, ai-seo, site-architecture, programmatic-seo, schema, content-strategy, aso |
| **cro** | Turning visitors into customers | cro, signup, onboarding, popups, paywalls |
| **copy** | Writing copy, emails, social, video, images | copywriting, copy-editing, cold-email, emails, social, video, image, sms |
| **paid-ads** | Running & measuring paid ads | ads, ad-creative, ab-testing, analytics |
| **growth** | Referrals, retention, partnerships | referrals, free-tools, lead-magnets, churn-prevention, community-marketing, co-marketing |
| **sales-gtm** | Selling & launching | revops, sales-enablement, launch, pricing, competitors, competitor-profiling, directory-submissions, prospecting, public-relations |

## Recommended first step

Start with the **strategy** advisor and have it set up your *product-marketing context*
(`.agents/product-marketing.md`). Every other advisor reads that first, so doing it once
makes all of them give much sharper, on-brand advice.

## Examples

- *"Strategy advisor: I'm launching a renovations booking tool, where do I start?"*
- *"Copy advisor: rewrite my homepage hero to be punchier."*
- *"SEO advisor: audit example.com and tell me the top 5 fixes."*
- *"Growth advisor: design a referral program for my service business."*
