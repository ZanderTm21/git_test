The system

From enquiry to paid job — hands-free

One connected machine handles the parts of your business that usually eat your evenings. Here's exactly what happens, and where the automation takes over.

1. Capture every lead, from everywhere

Facebook & Google ads, website forms, missed calls, WhatsApp and DMs all funnel into one system. The moment someone raises a hand, the machine is already on it — no lead ever sits unanswered.

2. Instant AI conversations that qualify

Your AI replies in seconds, in your tone of voice. It answers questions, asks the right qualifying questions (project type, budget, timeline, location), and keeps following up until the lead is ready — 24/7, even at 2am. Speed wins: replying in 5 minutes books nearly double the jobs of replying hours later.

3. AI estimator sends the quote

Trained on your real pricing and rules, the AI estimator produces accurate quotes and sends them automatically — with e-signature ready to go. No more pricing jobs at midnight or forgetting to send the quote.

4. The client books and pays — themselves

The lead picks a slot from your live calendar and pays a deposit on the spot. The job is confirmed and the money is in — without a single phone call from you.

5. The job hits the operatives' app

The confirmed job drops straight into the check-in / check-out app. Operatives clock on, upload photos, complete the work and get sign-off — all tracked, so you have full visibility without micromanaging.

6. Leads on tap (optional)

Our creative and ad team keeps the top of the funnel full — high-converting creative, campaigns and content — so the machine always has fresh jobs to process.
